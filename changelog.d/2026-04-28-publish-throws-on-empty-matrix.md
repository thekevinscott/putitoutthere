- Changed: **BREAKING: `publish` throws on an empty matrix instead of exiting clean.** The reusable workflow's `publish` step now fails red when the plan is empty, with `PIOT_PUBLISH_EMPTY_PLAN` in the message. Previously, an empty plan logged `info: publish: plan is empty; nothing to release` and returned `ok: true` — leaving consumers with green release runs that hadn't published anything. Skips belong at the workflow gate (the `if:` on the publish job that reads the plan job's matrix output), not in the publish step.
