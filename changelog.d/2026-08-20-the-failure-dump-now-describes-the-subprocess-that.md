- Fixed: the failure dump now describes the subprocess that failed rather than the sentence putitoutthere wrapped it in. Handlers catch the process seam's `ExecError` and rethrow a rendered message with it as `cause`, and the `$GITHUB_STEP_SUMMARY` dump was built from that message — so every failed publish reported `command: ""`, an empty stdout and `exitCode: -1`, and the tool's own stderr was replaced by putitoutthere's paraphrase of it. Where the paraphrase was wrong, the evidence that would have shown it was gone: this is why #617 took four runs to diagnose instead of one. The dump now walks the `cause` chain back to the `ExecError` and reports the real argv, exit status, stdout and stderr (falling back to the old shape for failures with no subprocess behind them, such as a preflight rejection). The `NODE_AUTH_TOKEN` bootstrap hint likewise keeps npm's stderr underneath it — the hint infers a cause from the *absence* of the package and never from the registry's stated reason, so a reader needs the registry's words to check it. Everything stays behind the same redaction the dump already applied. #617.
