- Fixed: an over-ceiling failure dump now says how much it dropped and keeps both ends of what it kept, rather than stopping mid-sentence. A stream that simply ends is indistinguishable from a tool that fell silent, and the two readings call for opposite responses; the diagnosis also lives at the **tail**, where the failing tool prints its error, so keeping only a prefix discards the one part worth reading. Output past the ceiling is replaced by a leading `[putitoutthere] capture ceiling reached: dropped <n> bytes` line followed by the head and tail of the stream. The banner leads rather than sitting at the cut, so it survives the job summary's head-only truncation — which would otherwise elide the very fact that something was elided. An ordinary verbose build stays whole and untouched — a cold `cargo publish --verbose` was measured at ~380KB of stderr, three orders of magnitude under the ceiling. #664.
