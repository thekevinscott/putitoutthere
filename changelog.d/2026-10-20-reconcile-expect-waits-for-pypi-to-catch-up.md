- Fixed: `reconcile --expect` (the `pypi-tag` job) no longer fails when PyPI has not caught up with an upload. PyPI's per-version endpoint (`/pypi/<name>/<version>/json`) can 404 for minutes after `pypi-publish` finishes, so a single read seconds later failed the job and cut no tag, and the next release re-planned the same version. `reconcile --expect` now re-reads that endpoint for up to about 3 minutes before it reports the version as not found. #777. (verified by: integration, e2e/CLI, unit/ubuntu-latest)
