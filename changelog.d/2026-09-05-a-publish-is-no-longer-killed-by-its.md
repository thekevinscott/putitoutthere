- Fixed: a publish is no longer killed by its own output. The process seam captured every subprocess through Node's `execFile`, which caps output at 1 MiB by default and, on overflow, does not truncate — it raises `ERR_CHILD_PROCESS_STDIO_MAXBUFFER` and `SIGTERM`s the child. A `cargo publish --verbose` on a cold dependency graph, a `maturin build` with a long compile, or an `npm publish` over a large platform family all clear 1 MiB routinely, so the release died partway through an upload that was proceeding normally and reported a failure the tool never produced — with the real stderr discarded, since the overflow error replaces it. Worse, the kill lands mid-`publish`, which is exactly where the [all-or-nothing guarantee](../notes/design-commitments.md) is least able to help: the registry may already have the artifact. Capture is now unbounded during the call and bounded after it, at 8 MiB per stream, so the tool always runs to completion and the engine still cannot be grown without limit by a runaway child. #664.
